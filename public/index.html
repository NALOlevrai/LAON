From Zero to Online

The simple path to host a Node.js game site, in three steps.

1

Secure your VPS

Open only ports 22, 80 and 443 with your provider's Ingress rules and iptables.

iptables -A INPUT -p tcp -m multiport --dports 22,80,443 -j ACCEPT
2

Nginx + free HTTPS

Nginx forwards laon-studio.com to your app, Certbot adds the Let's Encrypt certificate.

sudo certbot --nginx -d laon-studio.com
3

Node.js kept alive

PM2 runs your Express server 24/7 with auto-restart and logs.

pm2 start server.js --name laon-api
